Our approach

Every room begins as a question about how you want to live.

We draw slowly. Proportion before palette, daylight before fixtures. The result is a space that feels inevitable rather than decorated — quiet enough to live in for twenty years.

The studio

Small by choice.

We take on a limited number of projects a year so the people who drew your plan are the people on site when it is built. No handover to a delivery team, no surprises at snagging.

How it runs

Four stages, no mystery.

  1. 01

    Listen

    A long conversation, a site visit, and an honest budget range before anything is drawn.

  2. 02

    Draw

    Plans, elevations and material boards. You see the room before a single wall moves.

  3. 03

    Build

    Weekly site reports with photographs, so you always know what changed and what it cost.

  4. 04

    Hand over

    Styled, photographed and documented — plus a care guide for every finish in the house.
